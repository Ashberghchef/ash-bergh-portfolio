Deck Aspirations

After several seasons in yachting and falling in love with life at sea, I’ve found myself increasingly drawn towards the deck. Having become competent in both the galley and interior, I’d like to broaden my knowledge and experience, filling in the gaps and developing a more complete understanding of what it takes to run a yacht.

I’ve always enjoyed being part of the wider rhythm of life on board, and I love the idea of becoming someone who can turn their hand to just about anything on a yacht. Ultimately, I’d like the sea to remain a permanent fixture in my life, whether through my work or, one day, sailing a boat of my own.

I see this as the beginning of a new chapter, with the longer-term ambition of working towards becoming a captain. For now, I’m looking forward to learning the ropes, getting stuck in and discovering another side of an industry that has already given me so much.

My practical deck experience includes watchkeeping during long passages and anchor watches, applying navigation rules and collision avoidance procedures, including passing arrangements, right-of-way responsibilities and interpreting navigation lights at night. I have assisted with handling fenders, docking, anchoring and mooring balls, as well as deck maintenance such as washdowns, teak sanding and caulk trimmimg. My interior experience has familiarized me with standards that naturally carry over to exterior upkeep. I have also been involved in guest logistics, reporting defects, logging some maintenance and bookkeeping. My exposure to vessel engineering includes clearing AC filters and monitoring generators and battery loads, water levels and adjusting input and output respectively. I have a particular interest in engineering and would like my next step to include gaining practical experience in generator servicing and basic plumbing.

I still remain open to enquiries for select chef positions, including charters, rotational roles and quality private yacht or household positions, alongside opportunities to further develop my deck experience. In terms of permanent deck positions, I am interested in junior deckhand roles, while for shorter-term opportunities I would consider delivery crew, regatta support and winter positions. I would also welcome occasional daywork and short-term or relief contracts, where these can be accommodated around existing commitments. Likewise, should I secure a permanent position in either department, I would remain open to gaining experience in the other during periods of leave or time off. This could include taking on short-term private chef work alongside a permanent deck role, or gaining short-term deck experience during rotational leave from a chef position, where possible, always with my primary commitments and responsibilities taking precedence.
